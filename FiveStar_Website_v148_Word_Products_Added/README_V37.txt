Five Star Fast Food v37 updates
- Checkout has separate Order on WhatsApp and Order on Website buttons. Both save orders to the server/admin panel first. WhatsApp choice opens a prefilled message; customer must press Send.
- Customer order card is shown in cart drawer after checkout and offers cancellation for 30 minutes while status remains received. Server-side cancellation window is enforced.
- Cart remove label is shortened to Remove.
- Admin order notifications are available as orders in the admin panel. Automatic WhatsApp notifications are NOT included; they require a later WhatsApp Business Platform/API integration.
- Browser/device testing is still recommended before production use.
